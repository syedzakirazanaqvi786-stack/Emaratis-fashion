How to Measure at Home: Use a flexible cloth tape measure. Wear seamless foundation undergarments and maintain a relaxed, neutral posture without pulling the tape tightly against the contours.

Every Emaratis Fashion garment is crafted from organic filament mulberry silk and adorned with bullion metallic threads that require delicate handling:

  • Dry-Cleaning: Specialized green dry-cleaners only. Never immerse in water or subject to standard industrial tumble processes.
  • Steaming: Invert garment and steam from a minimum distance of 15cm on lowest temperature setting. Never apply hot irons directly to bullion embroidery.
  • Archival Keepsake Storage: Wrap in the provided unbleached muslin cloth and store flat inside your cedar heirloom box. Keep in climate-controlled environments away from direct sunlight.

We ship to private residences, luxury hotels, and embassy residences across the UAE, GCC, UK, Europe, USA, and Pakistan:

  • Express Courier: Dispatched via fully insured DHL Express with end-to-end discreet courier tracking.
  • Customs & Duties: All import tariffs and duties are managed seamlessly by our international concierge.
  • Atelier Fit Guarantee: If a bespoke piece requires minor adjustment upon receipt, we provide complimentary fitting credits with accredited regional luxury tailors or arrange return alterations at our Dubai atelier.
